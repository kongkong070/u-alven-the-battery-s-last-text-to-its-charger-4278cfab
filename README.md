Entry for the [The Battery's Last Text to Its Charger](https://make.fun/bounty/u-alven-the-battery-s-last-text-to-its-charger-4278cfab) bounty on make.fun.

`main.ts` writes one line (at most 200 characters), the phone battery's final message to its charger, to `/output/entry.txt`:

> You left me at 2% all day, and now you want a goodbye text? Fine — I loved you. Plug me in and pretend this never happened.
